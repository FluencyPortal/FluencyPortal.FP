📚 Part A: Concept & Full Examples

1️⃣ Advanced Collocations

🔹 bridge the gap
Definition: To reduce or connect the division between two things or groups.
💡 Example: "We need to bridge the gap between our plan and what the team is actually doing."
🔹 reach a compromise
Definition: To reach an agreement where both sides make concessions.
💡 Example: "After two hours of discussion, we finally managed to reach a compromise."
🔹 make a sound decision
Definition: To make a sensible, reliable, and well-thought-out choice.
💡 Example: "To make a sound decision, you have to analyze all the data first."
🔹 address the root cause
Definition: To find and fix the fundamental source of a problem rather than just the symptoms.
💡 Example: "Instead of fixing small errors, we must address the root cause of the problem."

2️⃣ Semi-Fixed Expressions / Sentence Frames

🌟 The main reason why... is that...
💡 Example: "The main reason why our sales dropped is that we didn't market the product well."
🟣 When it comes to..., I strongly believe that...
💡 Example: "When it comes to team productivity, I strongly believe that clear communication is key."
🟣 The way I see it, the key factor here is...
💡 Example: "The way I see it, the key factor here is how fast we adapt to changes."

📖 Reading & Context: The Case Study

Slide 1 of 2

📖 Read the story and observe how all chunks work together:

"Last year, our team was facing a huge dilemma at work. When it comes to launched projects, I strongly believe that clear communication is everything. However, we had communication gaps that posed a challenge to our deadline.

The main reason why our project was delayed is that we failed to address the root cause early on. The way I see it, the key factor here was that no one took the time to smooth out the differences between the departments.

Thankfully, we had a quick meeting to bridge the gap. After some discussion, we managed to reach a compromise and make a sound decision to adjust our workflow. Now, everything is running smoothly!"

🗣️ 10 Deep Discussion & Practice Questions

Slide 2 of 2

Answer the following questions using target chunks:

  1. What was the primary conflict mentioned in the story?
    💡 Frame requirement: Use "The main reason why..."
  2. What was the critical mistake the team made at the beginning?
    💡 Target Chunk: "address the root cause"
  3. According to the narrator, what is the most vital element in project launches?
    💡 Frame requirement: Use "When it comes to..., I strongly believe that..."
  4. How did the team resolve the conflict between departments?
    💡 Target Chunks: "bridge the gap" & "reach a compromise"
  5. In your opinion, what makes a decision a "sound decision" in business?
    💡 Frame requirement: Use "The way I see it, the key factor here is..."
  6. Recall a time at work/study when you had to "bridge the gap" between two conflicting opinions. What happened?
  7. Why do many teams treat symptoms of a problem rather than "addressing the root cause"?
  8. Is it always easy to "reach a compromise"? What should you do if someone refuses to compromise?
  9. "When it comes to career success, I strongly believe that consistency beats talent." — Do you agree or disagree with this statement? Explain.
  10. "The way I see it, the key factor to improving fluency is speaking without fear of making mistakes." — How do you work on this personally?

🟢 (No unvoiced /θ/ in this sentence)
🔵 Their, mother, father, together, with, them
🟠 mother and ➔ (mo-ther-and) | with them ➔ (wi-them - assimilation/merging)

🟢 think, three
🔵 They, those, brothers, there, together
🟠 think those ➔ (think-those) | went there ➔ (wen-there - dropping the /t/)

5️⃣ Guided Chunk Challenge

Slide 5 of 7

📚 Part A: Concept & Full Examples

1️⃣ Verb-Preposition Combinations / Fixed Phrases

🔹 at the expense of...
Definition: Done in a way that harms or sacrifices something else.
💡 Example: "You shouldn't focus entirely on your career at the expense of your health."
🔹 out of hand
Definition: Out of control or impossible to manage.
💡 Example: "If workload gets out of hand, you need to speak with your manager immediately."
🔹 on a regular basis
Definition: Happening frequently and with consistent routines.
💡 Example: "Taking short breaks on a regular basis helps keep your focus sharp."
🔹 cater to...
Definition: To provide what is needed or wanted by a particular group or situation.
💡 Example: "Modern companies should cater to the needs of employees who want remote work options."

2️⃣ Semi-Fixed Expressions / Sentence Frames

🌟 From my perspective, the most effective way to... is...
💡 Example: "From my perspective, the most effective way to avoid burnout is setting clear boundaries."
💜 It goes without saying that...
💡 Example: "It goes without saying that working 12 hours a day will harm your productivity."
💜 As far as I'm concerned, ... plays a huge role in ...
💡 Example: "As far as I'm concerned, time management plays a huge role in reducing daily stress."
